# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want technical disagreement to be candid, useful, and humane. Contributors
must communicate respectfully, assume good intent while evaluating evidence,
and make room for people with different backgrounds, languages, identities,
and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban participants whose conduct harms the community.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters.

To report conduct privately, contact the repository owner through
<https://github.com/bilal-> and request a private channel. Do not include
sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as reasonably possible.
